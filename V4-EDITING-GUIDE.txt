VERSION 4 EDITING GUIDE

EASIEST LOCAL METHOD
1. Open content/settings.json in VS Code.
2. Change only the words between quotation marks.
3. Press Command + S.
4. Your Live Server preview refreshes automatically.

ONLINE NO-CODE METHOD
Visit /admin/ on the live Netlify site. The editor is organized into Couple, Wedding Details, Colors, Home Page, Homepage Journey, Our Story, Wedding Weekend, Travel & Local Guide, FAQ, Registry, RSVP, and Photos.

PHOTOS
You may upload photos in the online editor. For local edits, place a photo in assets and change its path in content/settings.json. Example: /assets/new-hero.jpg

PUBLISHING
Once the folder replaces the files in GitHub, Netlify will redeploy automatically.
